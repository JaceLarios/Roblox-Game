# Workshop background
Use case: stylized-concept. Production background asset for a Roblox collector's garage menu, landscape 1536x1024. A bright inviting cartoon automotive workshop interior, glossy turquoise blue floor, cobalt steel roof rafters, soft warm hanging lamps, tool shelves and tires confined to far left and far right edges, big softly illuminated garage windows. Eye-level frontal one-point perspective. High-quality playful hand-painted 3D cartoon game illustration with polished materials, crisp appealing shapes and rich cyan/blue colors, golden light accents. Center and lower two thirds spacious and visually quiet for live game item previews to be overlaid. Background environment ONLY. Absolutely no GUI, no panels, no buttons, no platforms, no vehicles, no people, no logos, no lettering, no text. It will be reused beneath transparent blue menu panels, so keep the center soft and low contrast while edges carry workshop detail.

# Pet portraits
Use case: stylized-concept. Production pet portrait sprite sheet for a Roblox garage collector interface. Transparent PNG 1536x1024, exactly 3 equal columns and 2 equal rows, six separate full-body pet illustrations centered in each cell with 12% empty padding and no overlapping cells. Glossy finely detailed 3D cartoon cute toy animals, bold navy outlines, sparkling blue mechanical eyes, copper and turquoise salvaged metal panels and small bolts, appealing clean premium game icon polish. Top row: friendly cream-and-copper robot dog with floppy ears and blue face display; elegant teal copper robotic parrot with spread segmented wings; green mechanical turtle with riveted patchwork shell. Bottom row: orange copper mechanical bunny with long upright ears; translucent glowing lavender ghost kitten with playful face; orange white and teal robot fox with fluffy segmented tail. All creatures face three-quarter toward viewer. No text, no numbers, no nameplates, no background, no floors, no pedestals, no UI. Genuinely transparent space surrounding each creature.

Final pet iteration requested a transparent three-by-two sprite atlas of isolated mechanical dog, parrot, turtle, rabbit, purple ghost cat and fox, using copper/teal/ivory materials and bright friendly faces.
